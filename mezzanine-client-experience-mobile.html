Comfort & everyday use
The library and lounge share air and sound. If private calls or acoustic separation are a priority, discuss an enclosed workspace. The washroom is accessed through the private dressing area; a guest-access alternative should be reviewed if entertaining is important.
What we will confirm together
Measured site dimensions; roof and mezzanine structure; book-storage loads; stair and guarding details; ventilation and cooling; electrical and lighting layouts; wet-area drainage; material samples and an itemised scope. These are design-development items for the project team.
